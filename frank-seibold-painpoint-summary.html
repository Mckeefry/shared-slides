Four Core Pain Points Behind Low Adoption

The Assistant answers deal-level lookup questions well — managers need aggregation, comparison, and orchestration instead

"Shadow Reporting" Burden

Managers manually reconcile SFDC (operational) vs. OSBO (financial) data — the systems don't align and lack matching granularity.

Root cause: PMG vs. Sub-BE mismatch.

Produces "false attrition" — migrated/consolidated/re-papered revenue miscounted as churn — and a trust gap where leadership debates data validity instead of strategy.

AI does not help with this today.

Invisible GTM Execution

Managers cannot see whether GTM initiatives — e.g. the Umbrella → Secure Access migration — are actually being executed by their team.

No way to measure growth-motion attach rates or specialist-level initiative execution.

Admin Overload vs. Risk Management

Sub-$50K deals get little/no risk scrutiny; opportunity note quality is inconsistent across specialists.

Managers manually research risk factors, producing subjective, hard-to-compare judgements and forecast inaccuracy.

Process Ambiguity & Redundancy

Opportunities are "touched" by both the renewal team and the Renewal Center with no clear ownership/routing rules.

The burden of resolving overlap falls on managers; existing prompts to flag Renewal Center involvement are limited by poor underlying data.

Cross-cutting theme: Frank wants the Assistant to act as a "thought partner" — a 24/7 chatbot consultant that coaches managers through deal assessments by asking questions, not just retrieving data.

False Attrition: The Agreed Priority #1

Revenue that migrated, consolidated, or was re-papered — but reports as churn

What's Actually Happening

A predecessor contract's revenue migrates to another product/SKU, gets consolidated, or is re-papered into a different contract vehicle — but there is no linkage between the predecessor and successor contract.

Why This Is Priority #1

The single explicit prioritization agreement from the 4 Sept meeting: the team agreed to prioritize resolving false attrition and developing true attrition visibility at the PMG level. Michael committed to escalating this within leadership.

It is the largest identified source of recurring manual work — reconciled by managers every cycle, then re-debated by leadership. It is the root cause of the trust gap undermining every other Assistant output, and resolving it is a prerequisite for measuring GTM execution.

Proposed KPI 1
Reduction in manual reconciliation hours per manager, per cycle
Proposed KPI 2
% of former attrition volume reclassified as migration/consolidation ("false attrition rate")
Proposed New Assistant Prompt
Show me everything currently counted as attrition that is probably false attrition, and explain why.
Expected output: a ranked list of at-risk attrition lines with evidence per line and a one-click confirm/override. Once confirmed as "migration, not attrition," the classification is written back and persists — never manually re-derived in a future cycle.
Companion agreed-priority prompt: "What is my true attrition at PMG level for [period], excluding migrations and consolidations?"

Recommendations & Next Steps

False attrition resolution is Phase 1 — the anchor investment already agreed in the 4 Sept session

Phase 1 · Build
Build the reconciliation/entity-resolution service behind the new prompts — compute in a deterministic service; the Assistant narrates/explains, it does not compute via the LLM.
Phase 1 · Define
Operational team first agrees the upstream definitions engineering can't invent: a standard reconciled SFDC/OSBO report, a versioned Sub-BE → PMG mapping, and a false-attrition classification taxonomy.
Phase 1 · Publish
Publish "true attrition at PMG level" as one standard, shared, leadership-facing artefact — replacing manager-by-manager spreadsheets.
Ongoing
Protect the reclaimed time — reinvest it explicitly into 1:1 coaching and proactive risk management, not new reporting asks.
Follow-Up Action 1
Present to the AI Adoption team (including their manager) to secure sponsorship and prioritization for Phase 1.
Follow-Up Action 2
Circle back to Frank Seibold to confirm these findings accurately reflect his intent before further scoping.
This is a proposal for prioritization, not a committed roadmap — false attrition was the one item the room agreed on; everything else here is a recommendation built from that agreement.
