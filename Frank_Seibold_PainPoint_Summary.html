False Attrition: The Agreed Priority #1

Revenue that migrated, consolidated, or was re-papered, but reports as churn

What Is Happening

Successor and predecessor contracts have no recorded linkage, so migrations appear as attrition.

Why Priority #1

The largest recurring source of manual reconciliation and the root cause of the trust gap.

Proposed New Assistant Prompt
Show me everything currently counted as attrition that is probably false attrition, and explain why.
