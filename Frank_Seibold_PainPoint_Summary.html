False Attrition: The Agreed Priority #1

Revenue that migrated, consolidated, or was re-papered, but reports as churn

What Is Happening

A predecessor contract can migrate to another product or contract vehicle with no recorded linkage to the successor.

This is reported as attrition even when the revenue should be recognized as a migration or consolidation.

Why This Is Priority #1

It is the largest recurring source of manual reconciliation and the root cause of the trust gap affecting every other Assistant output.

Proposed KPIReduction in manual reconciliation hours per manager, per cycle
Proposed KPIShare of former attrition reclassified as migration or consolidation
Proposed New Assistant Prompt
Show me everything currently counted as attrition that is probably false attrition, and explain why.

Expected output: a ranked list with evidence, confirmation or override, and a persisted classification.
